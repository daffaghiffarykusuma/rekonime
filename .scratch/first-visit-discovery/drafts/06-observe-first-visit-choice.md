# 06: Validate confident choice with occasional viewers

**What to build:** The product owner receives an evidence-based comparison of how occasional viewers choose a title in the current and revised experience, including comprehension and recovery problems rather than only save counts.

**Blocked by:** 02: Offer three understandable Discovery picks; 03: Skip and restore titles within a Discovery session; 04: Make lasting Taste Profile feedback deliberate and reversible; 05: Keep the chosen title visible with safe Undo.

**Status:** draft, pending breakdown approval

- [ ] Prepare a consistent choose-one-title task for the current baseline and the completed revised experience.
- [ ] Use occasional anime viewers unfamiliar with Rekonime's scoring, and record the actual participant context and procedure without inventing observations.
- [ ] Observe whether each viewer selects a title, explains its personal appeal, and understands the displayed episode and rating evidence.
- [ ] Include accidental temporary feedback, lasting feedback, and save reversal so recovery can be observed.
- [ ] Capture concrete confusion, hesitation, recovery, and unsupported interpretations; distinguish observation from the evaluator's inference.
- [ ] Keep the three-pick layout a hypothesis until evidence supports it; do not equate saved-title counts with confident choice.
- [ ] Report sample limitations and avoid unsupported numeric targets or improvement claims.
- [ ] Record engineering/browser verification separately from human usability evidence; this ticket does not replace per-feature tests.
- [ ] Actual participant access is an external prerequisite for completing observed validation. If unavailable, deliver the prepared protocol and explicitly leave observation and conclusions pending.
